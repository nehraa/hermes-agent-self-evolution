# Aidutech Outreach

Aidutech (OpenMAIC) is an AI-powered live classroom platform that brings personalized, interactive learning to students through simulated classroom environments. This skill covers product knowledge, explanation templates, and outreach approach for school sales.

## Product Brief

**Tagline:** "Your Professor, Debate Partners, and Tutor in One Session"

**What it is:**
An AI-powered live classroom platform that generates complete, interactive lessons featuring an AI professor, AI classmates, whiteboard, and quizzes — all working together in real-time.

**How it works:**
1. Student enters a topic (e.g., "Photosynthesis — Grade 10")
2. AI researches and generates scene outlines
3. AI creates agent profiles: 1 professor + 4 classmates (Skeptic, Builder, Creative, Examiner)
4. System generates scene content with dialogue and actions
5. Media generation (images/video where applicable)
6. TTS audio generation
7. Complete playable classroom session is ready

**Key Features:**

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| AI Professor | Teaches concepts with adaptive pacing |
| Agentic Classmates | 4 AI characters who debate, question, and explore — Skeptic challenges assumptions, Builder constructs explanations, Creative makes connections, Examiner tests understanding |
| Interactive Whiteboard | AI-assisted sketching and visual explanations |
| Adaptive Quizzes | Identifies weak areas and adjusts difficulty |
| Full Classroom Simulation | All elements combined for immersive learning |
| Teacher Dashboard | Analytics, class management, misconception detection |

**Scene Types:** slide, quiz, interactive, PBL (Project-Based Learning)

**Subjects:** All — Math, Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), History, Geography, Languages, and more

**Target Audience:** Ages 10+, middle school through adult education

## School Outreach Approach

### Discovery Questions
Ask before presenting:

1. "What are the biggest challenges your students face in understanding new concepts?"
2. "How do you currently identify when a student is struggling with a topic?"
3. "What tools are teachers using for homework or after-school learning?"
4. "What's your biggest constraint when it comes to personalized learning?"
5. "How do you currently track student progress across different topics?"

### Value Proposition by Role

| Role | Key Value |
|------|-----------|
| **School Principal** | Analytics for student performance, reduced teacher workload, improved outcomes |
| **Head of Academics** | Curriculum alignment, misconception detection, differentiated learning |
| **Teachers** | Time savings, dashboard insights, less grading, personalized support for struggling students |
| **Parents** | Affordable tutoring alternative, progress reports, safe learning environment |
| **Students** | Engaging lessons, debate-style learning, available 24/7 |

### Pitch Framework
1. **Hook:** "Most students don't struggle with intelligence — they struggle with getting explanations that match how they learn."
2. **Problem:** "Traditional learning is passive. YouTube is one-way. Tutoring is expensive."
3. **Solution:** "Aidutech creates an interactive classroom — professor + classmates + whiteboard — all AI-powered, available anytime."
4. **Proof:** "The AI doesn't just teach. It identifies exactly where a student is confused and adapts in real-time."
5. **CTA:** "Would you like a free trial for your teachers to test with one class?"

## Trial & Onboarding for Schools

1. **Set up teacher accounts** on the Teacher plan
2. **Select one class** to pilot (recommend 20-30 students)
3. **Assign one topic** per student to test (use curriculum-relevant content)
4. **Collect feedback** after 1 week: What worked? What was confusing?
5. **Review dashboard** with teacher: See analytics, misconception reports
6. **Expand** to full class or department based on results

**Success metrics to track:**
- Student completion rate
- Quiz improvement vs. baseline
- Teacher time saved on misconception identification
- Student engagement scores

## Product Brief

### Core Value Proposition
Aidutech generates complete, playable live classroom sessions — AI professor + 4 agentic classmates discussing, debating, and teaching any topic on demand.

### Platform Capabilities

**AI Agent Roles:**
- Professor Agent — delivers lesson, explains concepts
- Skeptic Agent — questions assumptions, deepens thinking
- Builder Agent — constructs arguments, reinforces learning
- Creative Agent — connects to real-world applications
- Examiner Agent — tests understanding, identifies gaps

**Interactive Elements:**
- Whiteboard — AI-assisted sketching, annotation, diagram drawing
- Adaptive Quiz Engine — identifies weak areas, adjusts difficulty
- Teacher Dashboard — class analytics, misconception heatmaps, progress reports

**Session Types:** Slide presentation, quiz mode, interactive Q&A, project-based learning (PBL)

**Subject Coverage:** Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, History, Geography, Languages — any topic user specifies

**Target Audience:** Ages 10+, middle school through adult education; decision-makers = school administrators, department heads, procurement officers

---

## How Sessions Are Created

**User Flow (6 Steps):**
1. Student or teacher enters topic — e.g., "Photosynthesis — Grade 10 Science"
2. System generates scene outlines from knowledge graph
3. AI creates agent profiles (professor性格, 4 classmate personalities)
4. Content engine generates dialogue + whiteboard content + quiz items
5. Media generator creates supporting images/video clips
6. TTS engine synthesizes audio; session becomes playable

**Technical Stack:** Multi-agent orchestration, RAG-based knowledge retrieval, custom LLM fine-tuned for educational dialogue, media generation pipeline

## Simple Explanation Templates

### For Non-Technical Administrators
> Aidutech creates AI-powered classroom sessions where students learn from an AI professor plus simulated classmates who debate and teach any topic. It generates these lessons automatically from a topic name — teachers don't build content from scratch.

### For Teachers
> Think of it as a lesson generator plus practice partners. You enter "World War I — Grade 9," and Aidutech creates a complete session: AI professor explaining causes, AI classmates debating the Treaty of Versailles, interactive whiteboard annotating the war map, adaptive quiz on key terms. Students get a structured, repeatable learning experience.

### For Parents
> Your child gets access to an AI tutor and study group available 24/7. When they type in a topic, the system generates a classroom-like session — an AI teacher explains the lesson, AI classmates discuss it, and there's a quiz to check understanding. It's like hiring a private tutor on demand.

### For Students
> You get your own AI teacher and study buddies. Type in anything you're learning — "Chemical reactions" or "Shakespeare" — and you can join a full class session immediately. The AI professor explains it, the classmates ask questions, and there's a quiz at the end. It's way more engaging than reading a textbook.

## Objection Handling Reference

**"Students might get wrong information"**
"The AI is grounded in educational knowledge graphs, not raw internet sources. Teacher dashboard shows misconception patterns across the class — if the AI consistently struggles with a concept, you'll see it flagged. Teachers can also customize session content before launch."

**"We can't verify quality"**
"We provide pre-built sessions for common curricula. For any custom topic, review the generated script and quiz before assigning. We also offer a 30-day pilot — run 3-5 sessions, then we review analytics together."

**"Our IT department is stretched thin"**
"Aidutech is browser-based — no installation required. SSO integration (SAML 2.0) connects to your existing identity provider in under an hour. Our onboarding team handles setup; IT involvement is minimal."

**"How do teachers actually use it?"**
"Enter topic → system generates session → teacher reviews script (optional) → students join with a link → session plays automatically → results populate dashboard. A teacher can set up a session in under 2 minutes."

## School Procurement Workflow

**Step 1 — Interest Assessment (Day 1-3):**
- Identify decision-maker (principal, curriculum director, or department head)
- Qualify fit: school size, subject areas, class structure
- Schedule discovery call (15 min)

**Step 2 — Demo + Needs Alignment (Day 5-10):**
- Deliver tailored demo covering relevant grade/subject
- Identify specific use cases (homework support, substitute coverage, exam prep)
- Quote based on teacher + student seat count

**Step 3 — Pilot Proposal (Day 10-15):**
- Capture school goals in writing
- Propose 30-day pilot with 10-20 students
- Outline success metrics (session completion rate, quiz accuracy, teacher time savings)

**Step 4 — Pilot Execution (Days 15-45):**
- Setup teacher accounts + SSO integration
- Provide onboarding session for teachers
- Weekly check-ins; collect feedback
- Mid-pilot adjustment if needed

**Step 5 — Evaluation + Contract (Days 45-60):**
- Present pilot data: active sessions, student engagement, learning outcomes
- Draft contract with pricing locked for 12 months
- Route to procurement; handle RFP requirements if required

**Step 6 — Close + Handoff (Days 60-90):**
- Contract signed
- Bulk license activation
- Teacher training session
- Ongoing account manager assigned

# Abhinav Nehra — Life Story

*Transcribed from voice, May 4, 2026. Comprehensive personal history — reference when context about his background, values, or journey is needed.*

## Early Years — The Explorer

- Father served in the military, family relocated frequently
- Constant exposure to different cultures and environments
- Felt constrained by family structure — craved freedom to explore independently
- This explorer's mindset remains deeply engrained in his character

## Fourth Grade — The Spark

- Sister brought home a circuit project (simple light-up circuit)
- Father soldered it together
- Immediate fascination: the ability to control something with just a battery
- Began experimenting — removed battery, inserted random objects between contact points
- Continued until battery was completely drained
- Sister was frustrated by the ruined project
- Father responded by buying Abhinav his own soldering iron and components
- Father worked in military communications — servers, fiber optics, radio, cellular systems
- Around age 10, father took him to a server room
- Described it as "the most beautiful thing I've ever seen" — white floors, 50 servers with blinking lights, air conditioning running at full capacity

## Fifth to Seventh Grade — The Tinkerer

- Accumulated a shoebox dedicated to salvaged electronics
- Every broken electronic device: opened it, extracted usable components into the box
- Daily routine: open the box, combine random parts
- Internal dialogue: "What if I connect this, this, and this?"
- Experiments often took place in the garden to avoid disturbing others
- Sometimes capacitors would burst — still loved it

## Eighth Grade — The Computer and the Question

- Received his first computer
- First action: installed Call of Duty, played through and finished the game
- Then the pivotal question emerged: how was this made? What makes it work?
- Childhood approach to toys: given a toy, opened it next day, sometimes functional, sometimes not
- "If it doesn't work anymore — yippee, I just got a radio antenna. Can make a transmitter."
- Discovered Unity game engine
- Spent entire weekends watching YouTube tutorials, building projects
- Core belief formed during this time: *"If you can think it, you can make it"*
- Used the empty Unity scene as a metaphor: you can create literally anything, control gravity, speed of sound, speed of light — no boundaries

## School Years — The Reluctant Student

- Was never a "bright" student — only above average
- Struggled with rote memorization
- Stronger skill: synthesizing ideas across different domains
- Felt the education system wasn't designed for this thinking style
- **10th Grade:**
  - Performed very poorly in mathematics
  - Passed by the minimum margin
  - Mathematics teacher publicly humiliated him: *"I can guarantee you won't pass 10th grade"*
  - Fueled by anger
  - Studied intensively and passed
- **12th Grade:**
  - Chose the PCB stream (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Computer Science)
  - Recognized as a demanding academic track
  - Passed successfully
  - COVID disrupted final examinations
- Primary motivation: proving her wrong

## Shooting — Calm Under Pressure

- Practiced 10-meter air pistol shooting during 9th-10th grade
- COVID-19 pandemic forced him to quit
- Reached national competition level within one year of starting

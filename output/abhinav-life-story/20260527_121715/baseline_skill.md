# Abhinav Nehra — Life Story

*Transcribed from voice, May 4, 2026. Full story dump — reference when context needed.*

## Early Years — The Explorer
- Dad in military, moved around a lot. Traveled, loved experiencing cultures.
- With family felt couldn't explore enough, but now can. Explorer's nature still engraved.

## Fourth Grade — The Spark
- Sister had circuit project (lights up). Dad soldered it.
- Fascinated — could turn it on/off, just a battery. Started experimenting: battery out, put things between holes, used it until it died.
- Sister was pissed. Dad got him a soldering iron, started buying him stuff.
- Dad worked in military communications — servers, fiber optics, radio, cell phones.
- Took him to a server room at ~10 years old. "Most beautiful thing I've ever seen" — white floor, 50 servers, blinking lights, ACs on full.

## Fifth to Seventh — The Tinkerer
- Had a shoebox. Every broken electronic — opened it, extracted parts into box.
- Every afternoon: open box, merge random parts. "What if I connect this, this, and this?" Then built it.
- Went to garden to avoid waking anyone / capacitors bursting.
- Loved it.

## Eighth Grade — The Computer and the Question
- Got computer. First: installed Call of Duty, played and finished it.
- Then: how was this made? What makes it tick?
- Kid ideology: give a toy, opens it next day, sometimes works, sometimes doesn't. If doesn't — yippee, got a radio antenna. Can make a transmitter.
- Got into Unity. Sat entire weekends watching YouTube tutorials, building things.
- "If you can think it, you can make it." Canvas metaphor: empty Unity scene, can make literally anything — control gravity, speed of sound, speed of light.

## School — The Reluctant Student
- Not a bright kid. Above average only. Bad at memorizing.
- Better at merging ideas from different domains. Education system not built for this.
- 10th grade: really shitty at math, passed barely by a number. Math teacher humiliated him: "I can guarantee you won't pass 10th."
- Furious. Studied. Passed.
- 12th grade: took PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Math, Computer Science). Hard stream. Passed. COVID disrupted exams.
- Motivation: prove her fucking wrong.

## Shooting — Calm Under Pressure
- 10 meter air pistol, 9th-10th grade. COVID forced quit.
- Reached nationals in one year. Never qualified again.
- Taught calm and pressure handling.

## Going to the US
- During COVID, sister and he talked: try US, maybe scholarships.
- Prepared for SATs. Knew couldn't do JEE. US option seemed better.
- Landed at 17.

## College in the US — The Fall
- First semester: fun, diverse, nice.
- Then isolation, depression.
- Friend reached out, group accepted him. Things better. Better. Better. Better.
- End of second semester: best time ever.
- Group said: "too much drama around you, can't be friends." Done.
- Became paranoid on small campus. Couldn't show face. Stopped eating for 2 days. Couldn't leave room.

## COVID Delhi
- Close friend in same building. Same neighborhood. Spent lots of time together.
- Friend went to NJ campus. He went to IL campus, one week later.

## Transfer to New Jersey
- Friend suggested transfer if things that bad. He said: less prestigious uni, but if I stay I don't think I'll live.
- Transferred. By this time on anti-depressants.

## New Jersey — Best and Worst
- Fourth semester: really good. Friend got girlfriend. Friend became shitty to GF, she vented to him, friend got paranoid something going on between them.
- Told him multiple times: nothing going on, sit down. He didn't listen.
- Stopped talking entirely. Same room, texted each other. That bad. With someone who was one of his best friends.

## Mirror Moment — Choosing to Change
- Second time things fell apart. Stood in mirror.
- "I'm not enough. Barely passed 10th. Teachers said I wouldn't do anything in life. Maybe they're correct."
- Two choices: keep crying I'm not enough. OR make a fucking list of what to do to be enough.
- Picked B. Started self-improvement. Got into stoicism.
- Knows perfection doesn't exist but still walks the path.

## The Hero Visualization — As a Kid
- Made up a character in mind before bed. dissociation technique (later caused issues).
- Character: smart, strong, kind. Loves friends. Fights for what's right. Has morals. Even if costs everything. Brave. If loses, if dies — not a single tear. Lives life to the fullest.
- That's the person he wanted to be. Life shaped him that way.
- "First step towards perfection is knowing there is no perfection. And still walking the path. Something is better than nothing. If perfection is a marathon, most people aren't even trying because they think it has no end. Why? At least try."

## The Mask — And Taking It Off
- Realized he'd been wearing a mask his whole life. Analyze group for a week. Figure likes/dislikes. Custom personality per person.
- Exhausting.
- Later: certain people, didn't need to wear mask. Just him. They accepted him.
- Realized: true him is not so bad. People like him. Doesn't have to perform to be accepted.

## Research Phase
- Sixth semester: CS research. Library, 10 hours at a stretch.
- Forgot to eat, forgot to drink water. 2 hours pass, look up — sun setting. 10 hours felt like 1 hour. Flow state.
- This was when Pan-Gear Foundation idea came.

## Montana — Meeting Tyler
- Summer: volunteering at hostel in Bozeman, Montana.
- At such places: either people who did big things and walked away from money/fame. Or people trying to find themselves. Or people who found themselves and didn't want typical life.
- Every person there was inspiring. "You are the average of the 5 people you sit with."
- Met Tyler there — still his best friend.
- Leaving was hard as hell.

## The Pattern
- This always happens: whenever things go bad, next time good = immensely good.
- Lower the water goes back, bigger the wave of prosperity. Low value, high peak.

## After Montana — Downward Spiral Again
- Roommate: scary, threatening. Told RA. RA did nothing (political dynamics).
- Moved off campus. Helped housemates a lot, treated them like family.
- Girl got pregnant. Mood swings. They fucked him over. Told him: get out within a week.
- After he paid rent for next month + deposit.
- Within a week: find a place or homeless.
- Somehow moved back on campus.
- Thanksgiving: can't stay. International student.
- Said: I think I'm just going to come back home. I think I'm done.

## Leaving — Giving Up the Cat
- Left degree at 7th semester. Thanksgiving break. Flew home.
- Had to give up his cat. She was his life. Loved her more than himself.
- Hardest part of leaving.

## Back in India — The Rebuild
- First 2 months: depressing.
- Then: teaching illustrator job. Doesn't pay much. Stayed with family. Earning decent.
- Degree online. Likes teaching.
- Long game: professor in 5-6 years while doing PhD/postdoc. Safe base + side startups.
- Registered company. Most things 90% built. Actively executing.

## Now
- Executing. Building. Ready to start.
- Multiple ventures at 90%. The journey from falling to rebuilding has been the calibration.
